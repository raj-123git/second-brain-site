# Health routine and vegetarian meal planning

Health and diet as a running project: a standing assistant that holds my routine and plans a vegetarian week around it, adjusting when travel or work breaks the pattern. Nothing clinical, no numbers to chase; just a consistent plan that survives contact with a real schedule.

From [rajranpariya.com](https://rajranpariya.com/#artifact-health-routine). Personal tooling; mechanism only, no data.
